From first forecast to investor-ready financials — Ascenda covers the full lifecycle of your business plan.

5-Year Financial Forecasts

Build full P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow projections over 5 years. Model revenue from products, staff costs, capex, and operating expenses in one coherent plan.

Scenario Comparison

Run multiple scenarios side-by-side — base, optimistic, stress — and compare KPIs, margins, and cash burn across all of them without losing any data.

Break-Even Analysis Pro

Compute your BEP revenue and unit volume instantly. Run margin sensitivity tables, map your PCG cost structure, and create cost-optimisation plans to model the fastest path to profitability — with a multi-year BEP report aligned to your 5-year forecast.

Cap Table Management Pro

Track founders, investors, and ESOP pools in a live cap table with funding rounds, dilution waterfalls, and FastValo valuation scenarios. Planned capital increases in your Financial Plan automatically create a linked cap table round — keeping your financing plan and cap table in sync.

Snapshot & Audit Trail

Freeze any version of your plan as a snapshot. Compare versions over time, share with your accountant or board, and always know what changed and when.

Country-Aware Tax Rates

Select your country and Ascenda pre-fills VAT, corporate tax, and employer rates. Supports Belgium, France, Germany, UK, US, and 9 more out of the box.

Frequently asked

Quick answers to common questions.

Do I need a credit card to sign up?

No. The Free plan requires no payment information. You only need a credit card when upgrading to Pro.

Can I import existing data?

Yes. Ascenda's guided wizard lets you enter opening balances, historical headcounts, and product lines to populate your first plan in minutes. CSV import is on the roadmap.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Each tenant's data is isolated at the database level with row-level security. Ascenda is GDPR-compliant and hosted in the EU.

Can I collaborate with my team?

Yes. You can invite team members with owner or editor roles. Enterprise plans include advanced role-based access control.

How do the AI features work?

AI features read your plan data directly — no copy-pasting into ChatGPT. Each module (narration, unit economics, assumption review…) sends only the relevant numbers to the model and returns structured, editable output. Your financial data never leaves our EU infrastructure for training purposes.

Which business models are supported?

Ascenda ships with drivers for SaaS (MRR/ARR/churn), consulting & services (day-rate, utilisation), industry & manufacturing (units, COGS), training (sessions, seats), marketplace (GMV, take rate), and retail. You can also build custom formulas for any other model.
